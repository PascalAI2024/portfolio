# Public-Safe Publishing Guide

## Publish

- Problem framing, design decisions, and public product behavior.
- Screens, diagrams, mockups, and recorded interactions that contain no private data.
- High-level architecture and technical tradeoffs.
- Aggregated or rounded outcomes when they are truthful and approved.
- Recreated examples, synthetic data, and redacted workflows.

## Never publish

- Credentials, tokens, access links, configuration files, or deployment details.
- Client data, personal information, private messages, or unapproved brand material.
- Private source code, reusable automation recipes, security controls, or operational runbooks.
- Exact infrastructure topology, internal pricing, vendor agreements, or unpublished research.
- Claims that cannot be supported by the published evidence.

## Review before publication

- Is the story accurate without exposing private context?
- Does every image pass a human privacy and brand check?
- Are outcomes attributable, bounded, and honestly stated?
- Could a reader use the material to access, imitate, or compromise a private system?
- Has the relevant owner approved use of their name and material?

When in doubt, show the decision and the result — not the machinery.
