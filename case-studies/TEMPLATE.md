## The brief

language.

State the conditions that made the problem non-trivial. Include validation,
privacy, cost, platform, or operating constraints where they materially shaped
the work.

Explain the important technical or product decision and why it was chosen.

## The system shape

Use a high-level diagram when it improves understanding. Explain the boundaries
it intentionally omits.

## The evidence

Link the attributable result, validation artifact, live surface, source, or
learning. Keep the date and current project status beside the claim.

## The learning

State what the work established, including negative results or corrections.

## The boundary

into a public claim by implication.
